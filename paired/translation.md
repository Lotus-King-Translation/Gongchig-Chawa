Therefore, for those of noble lineage who continue their father’s line,

Excellent people who uphold the teaching and undertake dharma deeds [L016](../translations/NOTES.md#l016)

<!-- pair: GC-000169 -->
Because they are unbewildered about the uncommon, sublime heart [of the teaching] [L022](../translations/NOTES.md#l022)

To a retinue of kindred disposition and disciples of many kinds,
they teach well the distinct qualities of dharma without mixing them, [L024](../translations/NOTES.md#l024)

and proclaim a mighty, pleasing sound—thunder—in the ten directions. [L025](../translations/NOTES.md#l025)

Thus, from the profound, vast, unsurpassed
enlightened intent, wide as a great ocean,
this account, no more than a drop of water on a hair’s tip,
I, the wandering monk Sherab Jungne, have written. [L026](../translations/NOTES.md#l026)
